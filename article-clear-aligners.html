Orthodontics

Clear Aligners vs. Traditional Braces

Choosing the right orthodontic treatment is a significant decision. Both clear aligners and conventional braces are highly effective at straightening teeth, but they suit different lifestyles, treatment goals, and patient preferences.

Conventional Braces

Traditional metal or ceramic braces use brackets and wires to gradually shift teeth into alignment. They are highly effective for complex bite issues and severe crowding. Because they are fixed in place, compliance is guaranteed.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners offer a virtually invisible alternative. They consist of custom-made, clear plastic trays that you swap out every few weeks. Aligners are removable, making it much easier to brush, floss, and eat your favorite foods without restriction.

Which is Right for You?

The choice between aligners and braces depends heavily on your clinical needs and daily habits. During your consultation, we evaluate your specific case to recommend the most effective and comfortable path to your perfect smile.
