Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what patients ask most before booking a treatment.

How do I know which dental treatment is right for me?

Book a consultation at our clinic and our dentist will examine your teeth, discuss your concerns, and recommend the most suitable treatment plan for your situation.

Do you offer emergency dental treatment in Bengaluru?

Yes — call us directly for urgent issues like severe pain, swelling, or a broken tooth, and we'll do our best to see you the same day at our clinic.

How long does a typical dental treatment take?

It varies by procedure — a routine checkup or teeth whitening takes under an hour, while treatments like dental implants or orthodontics are completed over multiple visits.

Is anesthesia used during dental procedures?

For procedures like root canal treatment or extractions, local anesthesia is used to keep you comfortable and pain-free throughout the treatment.
