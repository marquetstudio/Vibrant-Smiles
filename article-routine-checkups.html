Preventive Care

The True Importance of Bi-Annual Checkups

Regular dental visits are essential for maintaining healthy teeth and gums. While brushing and flossing at home are critical, they aren't enough to prevent all oral health issues. Professional checkups allow us to identify problems early, when they are easiest and most affordable to treat.

What Happens During a Routine Checkup?

During your visit, Dr. Priya will conduct a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and mouth. We look for signs of decay, gum disease, and other oral health issues. Modern digital X-rays may be taken to see what's happening beneath the surface.

  • Professional Cleaning: Removal of plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing misses.
  • Oral Cancer Screening: A vital check for early signs of oral cancer.
  • Personalized Advice: Tailored tips for improving your daily oral hygiene routine.

We recommend scheduling a checkup every six months, though some patients may need more frequent visits based on their specific oral health needs.
