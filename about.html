Dr. Karthiyayini Priya

BDS, Certificate in Advanced Healthcare Management IIM-AIIMS Nagpur

With over 22 years of clinical excellence, Dr. Karthiyayini Priya is the driving force behind Vibrant Smiles Dental Clinic. She believes that oral health is an integral part of overall well-being, keeping pace with advancements in modern dentistry to improve treatment precision, patient comfort, and clinical outcomes.

Dr. Priya focuses extensively on comprehensive dental care, preventive interventions, restorative treatments, and oral health education. Her core specialties lie in Pediatric and Aesthetic Dentistry, ensuring that children, adults, and senior citizens all receive specialized, age-appropriate care in a relaxing environment.

"Our philosophy is simple: Specialized dentistry with personalized care. Every patient receives a transparent treatment plan explained thoroughly to reduce anxiety and predict successful outcomes."
